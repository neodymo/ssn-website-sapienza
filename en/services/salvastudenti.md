# SalvaStudenti

The SalvaStudenti service was created to offer strategic support to students who are behind schedule or encountering specific difficulties in passing their **first-year exams**, through a targeted tutoring program.

{{% hint tip %}}
<i class="fa-solid fa-lightbulb" style="color: #238636;"></i> **Who is it for?**

The service is dedicated to students enrolled in the Bachelor's Degree programs in **Computer Science** and **ACSAI** who still need to pass the exams required by the first-year study plan.
{{% /hint %}}

## How it works

The service is based on **tutor-coordinated study groups**, aiming to help prepare for exams in a practical and collaborative way.

* **Delivery mode:** meetings take place both online (via digital platform) and in person, adapting to the operational needs and schedules of participating students.
* **Meeting frequency:** sessions are scheduled on a weekly or periodic basis, ensuring the continuity required to cover each subject's syllabus.
* **Methodological approach:** tutoring sessions cover complex concepts, practical exercises, and practice exam simulations, encouraging ongoing discussion between tutors and students.

## How to apply

To join the program, you must fill out the [online application form](https://forms.gle/2AxXr6UjGC4W1tdQ6), specifying in detail which courses you encounter the most difficulty with and require support for. Once applications are processed, tutors will structure the groups and provide you with instructions on how to get started.

{{% hint warning %}}
<i class="fa-solid fa-triangle-exclamation" style="color: #FFD43B;"></i> **Course limit**

Each student can select a maximum of **three courses**.
{{% /hint %}}
